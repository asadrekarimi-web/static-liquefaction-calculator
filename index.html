Static liquefaction strength ratios — Sadrekarimi (2016) vs. other methods

Undrained yield, su(yield)/σ′vo, and liquefied, su(liq)/σ′vo, strength ratios from Sadrekarimi (2016) for compression, simple shear and extension, accounting for slope-induced anisotropy Kc, compared with Olson & Stark (2002), Idriss & Boulanger (2015) and Robertson (2010). Clean sand (FC = 0%) assumed throughout.
